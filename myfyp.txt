## Abstract {#abstract .unnumbered}

> Thyroid disorders are among the most prevalent endocrine diseases and
> are often associated with long-term complications affecting multiple
> physiological systems, particularly cardiovascular and metabolic
> functions. Interpreting thyroid laboratory reports can be challenging
> for patients, leading to delayed awareness of potential health risks.
> ThyroCheck proposes a mobile-based intelligent health risk assessment
> system that integrates Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Machine
> Learning (ML), and cloud-based backend services to analyze thyroid
> laboratory reports and provide multi-domain risk evaluation.
>
> The proposed solution is implemented as a cross-platform React Native
> mobile application that enables users to securely sign up and sign in,
> upload or scan thyroid laboratory reports in image or PDF format, and
> maintain historical health records. Firebase Authentication is used to
> manage secure user access, while Firebase Cloud Firestore serves as
> the backend database for storing extracted report values, prediction
> results, and user history in a structured and privacy-aware manner.
> Uploaded reports are processed on a Python-based backend, where OCR
> techniques are applied to automatically extract thyroid hormone values
> such as TSH, T3, and T4.
>
> The extracted data, along with demographic and lifestyle information,
> is fed into trained machine learning models to assess hypertension
> risk and estimate future health risk levels across three independent
> domains: cardiovascular risk, metabolic complication risk, and
> thyroid-related complication risk. Each risk is reported using
> categorical levels (Low, Medium, High) to ensure interpretability and
> clinical safety. ThyroCheck generates a comprehensive PDF report
> summarizing extracted laboratory values, risk predictions, and
> explanatory insights for the user.
>
> ThyroCheck is designed as a decision-support system rather than a
> diagnostic tool and incorporates ethical considerations related to
> data privacy and medical responsibility. This project demonstrates the
> effective integration of mobile application development, OCR-based
> data extraction, machine learning--driven risk assessment, and
> cloud-based data management to enhance early health risk awareness and
> accessibility to intelligent healthcare support solutions.

# Chapter 1: Introduction to the Problem {#chapter-1-introduction-to-the-problem .unnumbered}

#### Introduction

> Thyroid disorders are among the most common endocrine diseases
> worldwide and are frequently associated with other chronic health
> conditions, particularly hypertension, cardiovascular diseases, and
> metabolic disorders. Abnormal levels of thyroid hormones such as
> Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH), Triiodothyronine (T3), and
> Thyroxine (T4) can influence blood pressure regulation and metabolic
> balance, potentially leading to long-term complications. Early
> identification of hypertensive status and related health risks in
> patients with thyroid abnormalities is therefore crucial for timely
> intervention and prevention. Despite the availability of thyroid
> laboratory testing, many patients face significant difficulty in
> interpreting their reports and understanding whether they are
> hypertensive or non-hypertensive, as well as the potential health
> risks associated with their condition. Medical reports are often
> complex and require professional interpretation, which may not always
> be accessible during short clinical consultations. As a result,
> patients may remain unaware of both their hypertension status and
> their broader risk of developing cardiovascular, metabolic, or
> thyroid-related complications.
>
> In addition, thyroid laboratory reports are commonly stored as paper
> documents, scanned images, or PDF files, making manual analysis and
> long-term health tracking inefficient. Existing digital health
> solutions often lack automated report interpretation, hypertension
> prediction, and multi- domain risk assessment within a single
> integrated platform. Most systems focus on isolated conditions and do
> not provide a comprehensive overview of how thyroid disorders can
> simultaneously affect multiple physiological systems. With the growing
> adoption of mobile health technologies, there is a clear need for an
> intelligent, mobile-based decision-support system that can
> automatically extract thyroid hormone values from laboratory reports,
> predict whether a patient is hypertensive or non-hypertensive, and
> evaluate potential health risks across three independent domains:
> cardiovascular risk, metabolic complication risk, and thyroid-related
> complication risk. Addressing this problem through the integration of
> Optical Character Recognition (OCR), machine learning, and cloud-based
> services can enhance early health awareness, improve accessibility to
> personalized health insights, and support informed decision-making
> while maintaining ethical standards and data privacy.

#### Purpose

> The purpose of this project is to develop a mobile-based intelligent
> health assessment system that enables users to predict whether a
> patient is hypertensive or non-hypertensive based on thyroid
> laboratory data. ThyroCheck aims to simplify the interpretation of
> thyroid reports by automatically
>
> extracting key hormone values such as TSH, T3, and T4 using Optical
> Character Recognition (OCR) techniques. Another key purpose of the
> project is to apply machine learning models to predict hypertension
> status and to evaluate potential health risks across three independent
> domains: cardiovascular risk, metabolic complication risk, and
> thyroid-related complication risk. These risk outcomes are presented
> in categorized levels (Low, Medium, High) to ensure interpretability
> and to support user understanding without replacing professional
> medical diagnosis. Additionally, the project aims to provide a secure
> and centralized platform for managing personal health records. Through
> the integration of cloud-based authentication and database services,
> ThyroCheck allows users to securely store, track, and review
> historical thyroid reports, hypertension predictions, and multi-domain
> risk assessments. Overall, the purpose of this project is to enhance
> early health awareness, support informed decision-making, and improve
> accessibility to intelligent, technology driven healthcare
> decision-support solutions.

#### Objective

-   To Purpose a Preprocessing Method for Thyroid Hormonal dataset due
    to noise imaging.

-   To Propose a classification method to detect the patient as
    hypertensive or non-hypertensive and analyze the risk, issue was
    accurate identification.

-   To Propose an automatic report generation method the reason was
    manual interpretation.

#### Existing Solution

> Currently, there are several digital health applications and systems
> that assist patients in monitoring thyroid-related health and managing
> laboratory reports. Many mobile applications focus on tracking thyroid
> hormone levels or storing medical records, but they usually require
> manual input of laboratory data, which is time-consuming and prone to
> errors. Some platforms provide basic visualization of lab trends, but
> they do not offer automated interpretation or risk assessment for
> users. Other existing solutions focus on hypertension monitoring using
> wearable devices or manual data entry but rarely integrate thyroid
> hormone data into their analysis. Similarly, while some
>
> online healthcare portals offer risk calculators for cardiovascular or
> metabolic conditions, these tools are often generic, not personalized,
> and not integrated with real thyroid lab reports.
>
> Moreover, most current systems do not provide multi-domain risk
> predictions in a single platform, nor do they generate comprehensive,
> downloadable reports for the user. Integration of OCR for automatic
> extraction of lab values and machine learning for predictive analysis
> is largely absent in existing solutions, limiting usability and
> accuracy.
>
> In summary, existing solutions either focus on a single aspect of
> patient health, require manual data handling, or do not combine
> thyroid analysis with hypertension prediction and multi-domain risk
> assessment, leaving a significant gap for a fully integrated,
> intelligent, and mobile-based decision- support system.

#### Proposed Solution

> To address the limitations of existing systems, this project proposes
> a mobile-based intelligent healthcare decision-support system that
> integrates Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Machine Learning (ML),
> and cloud-based data management. The solution enables users to
> automatically extract thyroid hormone values (TSH, T3, T4) from
> uploaded or scanned laboratory reports and predicts whether a patient
> is hypertensive or non-hypertensive. In addition, ThyroCheck evaluates
> potential health risks across three independent domains:
> cardiovascular risk, metabolic complication risk, and thyroid-related
> complication risk, presenting results in Low, Medium, or High
> categories for clarity and interpretability.
>
> ThyroCheck is implemented as a cross-platform React Native mobile
> application, allowing users to securely sign up and log in through
> Firebase Authentication. Users can upload or capture laboratory
> reports in image or PDF format, which are processed on a Python-based
> backend. OCR techniques extract numerical values, which are then
> preprocessed and fed into trained machine learning models to generate
> predictions and multi-domain risk assessments.

# Chapter 2: Software Requirement Specification {#chapter-2-software-requirement-specification .unnumbered}

### Introduction

##### Purpose

> The purpose of this Software Requirement Specification (SRS) is to
> clearly define the functional and non-functional requirements of the
> ThyroCheck mobile-based intelligent health assessment system.
> ThyroCheck is designed to assist users in predicting whether a patient
> is hypertensive or non-hypertensive based on thyroid laboratory data
> and to evaluate potential health risks across three independent
> domains: cardiovascular, metabolic, and thyroid-related complications.
>
> ThyroCheck aims to simplify the interpretation of thyroid laboratory
> reports by automatically extracting hormone values (TSH, T3, T4) using
> OCR techniques, predicting hypertension status through machine
> learning models, and providing multi-domain risk assessments in Low,
> Medium, or High categories. Additionally, it ensures secure storage of
> user data, generates comprehensive PDF reports, and supports users in
> making informed health-related decisions as a decision-support tool
> while maintaining ethical and privacy standards.

##### Scope

> The scope of the proposed system includes the following:

-   Development of a **cross-**platform mobile application using **React
    Native** for analyzing thyroid laboratory reports.

-   Implementation of secure user authentication and account management
    through **Firebase**

> Authentication.

-   Automatic extraction of thyroid hormone values (TSH, T3, T4) from
    uploaded or scanned images and **PDF reports** using OCR techniques.

-   Prediction of the user's hypertension status (hypertensive or
    non-hypertensive) through trained **machine learning models**.

-   Assessment of **multi-domain health risks**, specifically:

    -   Cardiovascular risk

    -   Metabolic complication risk

    -   Thyroid-related complication risk

-   Presentation of risk levels in **Low, Medium, or High categories**
    for better interpretability.

-   Generation of comprehensive PDF reports summarizing extracted
    values, hypertension prediction, and domain-specific risk levels.

-   **Secure cloud storage** of user data, reports, and prediction
    results using **Firebase Cloud Firestore.**

-   Provision of a **decision-support platform** without functioning as
    a medical diagnostic tool, ensuring ethical compliance and data
    privacy.

> ThyroCheck focuses on providing **early health awareness and
> personalized insights**, enabling users to make informed
> health-related decisions. It does not replace clinical diagnosis or
> treatment but serves as an intelligent support tool for thyroid and
> hypertension monitoring.

    production and release of thyroid hormones.

-   **T3 (Triiodothyronine):** A thyroid hormone responsible for
    regulating metabolism and energy levels.

-   **T4 (Thyroxine):** A thyroid hormone that helps control metabolic
    processes in the body.

-   **Hypertension:** A condition in which blood pressure remains
    persistently higher than normal levels.

-   **Optical Character Recognition (OCR):** A technology used to
    extract readable text from images or scanned documents.

-   **Machine Learning (ML):** A computational approach that enables
    systems to learn from data and make predictions.

-   **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The capability of machines to
    perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

-   **Firebase Authentication:** A service used to securely manage user

-   **Firebase Cloud Firestore:** A cloud-based database used to store
    and manage application data.

-   **Decision-Support System:** A system designed to assist users in
    decision-making without providing medical diagnosis.

-   **Cardiovascular Risk:** The likelihood of developing heart or blood
    vessel-related complications.

-   **Metabolic Complication Risk:** The likelihood of developing
    metabolic disorders.

-   **Thyroid-Related Complication Risk:** The likelihood of developing
    complications related to thyroid dysfunction.

-   **React Native:** A framework used to develop cross-platform mobile
    applications using JavaScript and React.

-   **Flask:** A lightweight Python web framework used to build backend
    APIs.

-   **Firebase:** A cloud-based platform providing authentication,
    database, and backend services.

-   **Cloud Firestore:** A NoSQL cloud database for storing and
    synchronizing application data.

-   **Application Programming Interface (API):** A set of rules that
    enables communication between different software components.

# Overall description

##### Product perspective

> ThyroCheck is a self-contained mobile application built using React
> Native. It integrates Google Firebase for secure user authentication
> and cloud-based data storage, and a Python Flask backend for OCR
> processing and machine learning model training and inference. The
> machine learning models are trained as part of this project to predict
> hypertension status and assess multi-domain health risks. ThyroCheck
> is self-contained and does not rely on any other external software
> systems. All the interfaces and characteristics are given as follows:

##### System Interfaces

-   Firebase Authentication for managing secure user accounts.

-   Firebase Cloud Firestore for storing user profiles, extracted
    thyroid report values, hypertension prediction results, multi-domain
    risk assessments, and historical health records.

-   Python Flask REST API for OCR processing, trained ML model
    inference, and communication between the mobile app and backend
    services.

-   OCR Engine (Tesseract / EasyOCR) for extracting text from uploaded
    or scanned thyroid laboratory reports.

-   Machine Learning Models developed and trained within this project to
    predict hypertension status and multi-domain health risks.

##### User characteristics

> This subsection describes the general characteristics of the intended
> users of the ThyroCheck mobile application. The system is designed to
> be used by a diverse group of users with varying educational
> backgrounds, technical skills, and experience levels.
>
> The primary users of the system are patients who wish to analyze
> thyroid laboratory reports and receive health-related insights. These
> users may have basic to moderate educational backgrounds and limited
> technical expertise. Therefore, the application is designed with a
> simple and user- friendly interface that requires minimal technical
> knowledge to operate.
>
> Users are expected to have basic familiarity with smartphones,
> including the ability to install applications, upload images or PDF
> files, and navigate through mobile app screens. No prior medical or
> technical expertise is required to use the system effectively.
>
> Secondary users include system administrators who manage backend
> services, data storage, and system monitoring. Administrators are
> expected to have a higher level of technical expertise, including
> experience with cloud services, databases, and system maintenance.
>
> Overall, the system accommodates users with varying levels of
> experience by providing clear instructions, error messages, and guided
> workflows to ensure ease of use and accessibility.

##### Constraints

> This subsection provides a general description of the constraints and
> dependencies that may limit the developer's design and implementation
> choices for the **ThyroCheck** system. These constraints must be
> considered throughout the system development lifecycle.

##### Regulatory Policies {#regulatory-policies .unnumbered}

> The system shall comply with applicable data protection and privacy
> regulations. User medical data and personal information shall be
> handled securely, ensuring confidentiality and ethical use. Access to
> sensitive information shall be restricted to authorized users only.

##### Hardware Limitations {#hardware-limitations .unnumbered}

> The application shall operate on standard Android and iOS mobile
> devices with limited processing power and memory. Heavy computational
> tasks such as OCR processing and machine learning predictions shall be
> handled on the server side to minimize device resource usage.

##### Interfaces to Other Applications {#interfaces-to-other-applications .unnumbered}

> The system shall integrate with external services including Firebase
> Authentication, Firebase Database, Firebase Cloud Storage, and
> notification services. OCR and machine learning modules shall
> interface with backend APIs to process uploaded reports and return
> results to the mobile application.

##### Parallel Operation {#parallel-operation .unnumbered}

> The system shall support multiple users accessing the application
> simultaneously. Parallel operations such as concurrent report uploads,
> predictions, and PDF generation shall be managed securely without data
> conflicts or performance degradation.

##### Audit Functions {#audit-functions .unnumbered}

> The system shall maintain audit logs for critical operations including
> user authentication, report uploads, data access, predictions, and
> report downloads. These logs shall assist in monitoring system usage,
> detecting anomalies, and supporting maintenance activities.

##### Control Functions {#control-functions .unnumbered}

> Administrative control functions shall be provided to manage user
> access, monitor system activity, and oversee storage usage. Control
> mechanisms shall allow administrators to respond to system errors,
> high storage utilization, or abnormal user behavior.

##### Higher-Order Language Requirements {#higher-order-language-requirements .unnumbered}

> The system shall be developed using high-level programming languages
> and frameworks such as **JavaScript (React Native)** for the mobile
> application and **Python** for machine learning components. Backend
> services shall use modern, scalable technologies.

##### Signal Handshake Protocols {#signal-handshake-protocols .unnumbered}

> The system shall rely on standard network communication protocols for
> data exchange between the mobile application and backend services.
> Proper request-response mechanisms shall be implemented to ensure
> reliable data transmission and synchronization.

##### Reliability Requirements {#reliability-requirements .unnumbered}

> The system shall ensure high availability and consistent operation.
> Failures such as network interruptions or service downtime shall be
> handled gracefully, with appropriate user notifications and recovery
> mechanisms to prevent data loss.
