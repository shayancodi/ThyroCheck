# Privacy Policy for ThyroCheck

**Last updated: May 20, 2026**

## Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** Email and password for authentication (stored securely via Firebase).
- **Health Data:** Thyroid values (TSH, T3, T4) entered manually or scanned via OCR. This data is processed in real-time and is NOT stored on our servers.

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide thyroid health risk predictions.
- To authenticate your account.

## Data Storage
- Authentication data is managed by Firebase (Google).
- Thyroid values are processed in real-time and not permanently stored.
- Scanned images are processed for text extraction and immediately discarded.

## Third-Party Services
- **Firebase Authentication** (Google)
- **HuggingFace** (API hosting for ML predictions and OCR)

## Data Security
We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not intended for children under 13.
